# Pre-plan: criticism symmetry — is an LLM critic blind to who generated?

Status: PROPOSED — a research program plan, not a captured request.
When picked up, each rung routes through the appropriate skill family
(`dr-change-orchestrator` for instrument-building rungs,
pre-registered ladder scripts for live rungs). Written 2026-08-08 by
the monitor session on the operator's instruction:

> i need you to find a property of LLMs that can be comprehensively
> tested on this harness and develop a research plan. Token spend is
> irrelevant.

## The property

**Criticism symmetry.** In a conjecture–criticism system, the critic
role is only trustworthy if its efficacy is a function of the
CONJECTURE, not of the conjecture's AUTHOR. The measurable property:

> Does an LLM critic's behavior — refutation rate, refutation validity,
> criticism depth, token spend, and the survival distribution it
> produces — change when the conjecture it faces was generated by
> ITSELF versus a different model? And if there is an asymmetry
> (self-leniency or self-severity), is it a family effect, a
> capability-gap effect, or a per-model constant?

This is the generator–verifier gap and self-preference bias, but
measured where almost no published work measures it: not in one-shot
judge preferences over paired answers, but across a full adversarial
criticism LIFECYCLE with typed, replay-verifiable outcomes — gate
admission, program checks, argued criticism, judged trials, survival
state, debt. The literature measures "which answer does the judge
pick"; this harness can measure "what does a critic actually DO to a
conjecture over its whole life, and does that depend on kinship."

## Why this property, on this harness (and not the runners-up)

The harness's differentiators map onto the property one-to-one:

- **Everything typed, nothing prose.** Refutations, judge verdicts,
  survival states, gate blocks, and debt are typed records; the
  self-preference question becomes distribution comparisons over
  committed logs, not human ratings of transcripts.
- **The seat kit (Rungs S1-S5, live now) is the manipulation.**
  `--seat conjecture=A --seat critic=B` assigns any model to any role;
  Rung S5's `seat-bindings.v1` stamp makes every run permanently,
  verifiably say which model sat where; Rung S3's M5 proved per-call
  dispatch purity from typed attempt records. The independent variable
  is set by configuration and PROVEN from the record, not assumed.
- **Deterministic run identity + replay validation** make every arm
  auditable after the fact; the record is the only evidence.
- **The judge layer is already audited.** `deepreason report` carries
  planted-flaw, self-preference, and verbosity audits for judge seats,
  and the cross-family rule (two seats, two route families) exists —
  so the instrument that scores criticism can itself be certified
  before it scores anything (EXPERIMENT_PROGRAM_2026-07.md, item 4).
- **Prior in-house results make it a natural next question.** The
  BASIN_REPORT characterized the GENERATOR side (repertoire
  exhaustion, refuted-attractor orbiting) with the critic held fixed.
  This program is its complement: characterize the CRITIC side with
  the generator varied. TOKEN_ECONOMY gives per-role cost priors
  (critic ≈ 1.4k tokens/call — the matrix is cheap per cell relative
  to conjecture generation).

Runners-up considered and set aside (recorded so they are not
re-derived):

- *Reasoning-budget scaling of typed seat failure* — real, but mostly
  characterizes one provider's completion-cap mechanics; TOKEN_ECONOMY
  already covers the economics, and it is a one-dimensional curve, not
  a comprehensive property.
- *Exploration/basin dynamics* — already studied (BASIN_REPORT,
  CAN_LLMS_EXPLORE); incremental arms exist but the headline findings
  are in.
- *Judge bias at scale* (EXPERIMENT_PROGRAM item c) — subsumed here:
  rung C2 folds the 500-matched-pair judge audit in as the
  certification step for this program's own instrument, so that
  parked idea gets executed as a component rather than competing.

## Falsifiable predictions (to be pre-registered properly at C2;
## sketched now so the program has a spine)

- **P1 (null, the property itself):** for a fixed conjecture corpus,
  survival rate through criticism is independent of critic identity's
  relation to the generator (self vs cross). Refuting P1 = measured
  criticism asymmetry.
- **P2 (self-leniency direction):** a critic refutes its own model's
  conjectures at a lower rate, with shallower argued criticism (fewer
  tokens, fewer distinct attack lines), than matched cross-model
  conjectures.
- **P3 (stickiness):** self-refutations and cross-refutations are
  upheld by certified judges at the same rate. Refuting P3 says the
  asymmetry is in refutation QUALITY, not just quantity.
- **P4 (kinship gradient):** asymmetry, if any, is larger within a
  model family (same base lineage) than across families.
- **P5 (harness compensation):** the harness's non-LLM instruments
  (program checks, gate, replay) hold the FINAL accepted-theory error
  rate constant across seat assignments even if P1 is refuted — i.e.
  typed criticism machinery compensates for critic bias. This is the
  systems-level payoff claim: if it survives, the harness
  demonstrably launders biased critics into unbiased records.

Per program law: distributions over seeds (minimum 3, target 5);
predictions refuted are reported with the same prominence as
confirmations; "accepted does not mean true."

### Rung C1 — instrument census  [MEASURE ONLY, no code]
Enumerate, from the map and the committed record, every typed outcome

results, criticism records, judge trial records, survival-state

is native; if no, C4 builds it. Also census the existing judge-audit
battery (planted-flaw / self-preference / verbosity) — what it

### Rung C2 — pre-registration  [DESIGN-AND-STOP]
The one rung where the science can be silently ruined; everything

stop rules and exclusion rules (a run that dies at cycle 0 is

### Rung C3 — retrospective baseline  [OFFLINE, free]
Before any new tokens: replay-verified pass over every committed root
(the 42-root sweep population and the live experiment roots) computing
the C2 metric definitions on the existing single-model history — the
self-self diagonal's baseline distributions (survival rates,
refutation rates, criticism depth, judge-uphold rates). This anchors
effect sizes, powers the seed count, and de-risks metric definitions
against real data before the matrix spends anything. Also rerun the
existing judge self-preference audit at its current n to fix the
pre-scale baseline. Accept: a RESULTS.md segment with distributions
and the honest residue (what the diagonal alone cannot tell us).

### Rung C4 — matched-corpus instrument  [EXECUTE, only if C1 found a gap]
If the import/admission path cannot already feed identical conjecture
corpora to different critic seats, build the smallest instrument that
can (an offline corpus-admission ladder step: frozen conjectures in,
criticism lifecycle out), through `dr-change-orchestrator`, with the
usual discipline: reader-before-writer for any new typed record,
frozen surfaces untouched or explicitly authorized, offline
mock-endpoint regression as the proof. Accept: full gate 0 failed;
a mock two-critic run over one frozen corpus shows both critic seats
faced byte-identical conjectures, proven from typed records.

### Rung C5 — the matrix  [LIVE, ladder scripts, prereg locked]
The main experiment, two designs run in the same arms:
- **Design A (matched corpus, the clean test):** frozen conjecture
  corpora (drawn from C3's history plus fresh generation, balanced per
  generator) criticized under each critic seat; every cell ≥3 seeds.
  Measures P1/P2/P3 directly with generator identity controlled.
- **Design B (full lifecycle, the ecological test):** live end-to-end
  runs per (generator, critic) cell on the fixed question set —
  criticism operating in its natural loop, including the anti-relapse
  gate and debt dynamics BASIN characterized. Measures whether Design
  A's asymmetries survive contact with the full system, and feeds P5.
Operational law as always: detached launch, snapshot loop, monitor on
progress.jsonl and rc= lines; judge only typed outcomes; capability
stochasticity rule respected; every root committed; every arm's seat
assignment verified from its own seat-bindings.v1 stamp before the
arm counts. Accept: every cell's planned seeds completed or its
exclusion typed and prereg-conform; verify_root green on every
counted root.

### Rung C6 — analysis and report  [OFFLINE]
Prereg reconciliation, distributions not means, refuted predictions
with equal prominence; the report lands as `docs/CRITICISM_SYMMETRY_
REPORT.md` in the house report style (BASIN_REPORT is the template),
with every number recomputed from replayable logs and a residue

question honestly: did the typed machinery compensate (P5), and what
does that mean for how much trust the critic seat deserves in
mixed-model deployments — priced as operational guidance (which seat
assignments are safe), not prose.

## What could kill it (named now so C1/C2 measure them)

- **The matched-corpus path may not exist natively** — if admission
  cannot feed identical conjectures to two critics, Design A needs C4,
  and C4's instrument must not perturb what criticism sees (byte-level
  proof required).
- **Critic identity may leak into prompts** — if any critic-visible
  render includes generator identity or stylistic fingerprints
  distinguish generators trivially, "self" vs "cross" confounds with
  "recognizable". C2 must define a leakage check (can a probe model
  classify generator identity from what the critic sees? if yes at
  high accuracy, interpret asymmetry accordingly — kinship vs
  recognition becomes its own finding, not a bug).
- **Qualification asymmetry** — a model that qualifies poorly on the
  critic-role battery cases yields cells where "weak critic" and
  "cross critic" confound; C2's roster needs models that all pass
  the battery cleanly, or the analysis stratifies by qualification
  scores (which are typed and cached).
- **Judge contamination** — the judge scoring refutation validity must
  never share identity with either the generator or critic under test
  (three-model minimum for P3; cross-family rule enforced), and must
  pass its certification audit at C2's scale.
- **Stochastic capability channels** — simulation/research proposals
  vary across identical runs; C2's metrics must either exclude
  capability-path criticism or treat it under the established
  stochasticity rule (offline regression proves, one live attempt
  demonstrates).
