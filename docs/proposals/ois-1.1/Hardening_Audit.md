# Hardening audit: semantic authority and staged specification

## Assessment

**Both documents need hardening, but for different reasons.** `PopperSemanticsV1.md` has a substantially clearer semantic core than its implementation-oriented companion. Its principal formal defect is a missing contribution index in authorship. It also needs a more explicit contract preventing a refinement from converting a restricted evidence format into the definition of creativity. Its universal-capacity clause benefits from an explicitly identified strengthening concerning the achievement being attributed and what the enabling environment is allowed to supply.

`Open_Inquiry_Semantics_1_0(1).md` needs structural repair. It repeatedly states that its kernel is subordinate to its semantics, but several definitions contradict that commitment. Some exclude genuine cases the authority allows. Others allow an operational report to count evidence whose declared checks failed. Several worked fixture claims cannot be replayed from their own event and reference rules as written. These are not merely editorial problems.

The supplied replacements are `PopperSemanticsV1_1.md` and `Open_Inquiry_Specification_1_1.md`. They are proposed versions for adoption, not a claim that the user has already accepted a new authority. The originals are unchanged. The revised authority is standalone and contains no history of this audit. The revised specification binds explicitly to that authority and separates semantic relations from its executable bookkeeping component.

## Scope, evidence, and method

The two Markdown documents were read as the principal audit targets. The first was treated as authoritative for the second, while also being examined for its own gaps. Selected supplied book passages were checked where they discriminate an important repair: *The Fabric of Reality*, Chapter 3, printed pages 67–68; *The Beginning of Infinity*, Chapter 1’s account of explanation, variation and reach, and Chapter 16, printed pages 411–413; and the supplied EPUB chapters “Knowledge” and “A Journey There and Back Again” in Marletto’s *The Science of Can and Can’t*. This is not a claim to have re-audited every statement in all three books.

The tests are local, deterministic tests of finite representations and explicitly stipulated countermodels. No external model was used as an auditor, no current AI system’s creativity was measured, and no external repository was modified. The work does not establish a reductive theory of meaning or creative organization by placing names on the remaining substantive relations.

The source locations below use the supplied filenames and headings or section numbers so that they remain usable outside this chat. Historical claims about Popper and Bartley, and exact formal imports attributed to other researchers in the second document, were not independently verified against those primary works. They are therefore not retained as established normative premises in the replacement. The absence of a supplied earlier CR-1.0 schema is not filled with guessed definitions; the replacement states its own required contracts.

## What was preserved

The recursive semantic kernel remains problem-directed conjecture and criticism, including scrutiny of recognition, interpretations, standards, values, methods, comparisons, and attribution. It remains possible to act provisionally, stop an episode, reopen it, retain an explanation, reject a criticism, or expose a principled limitation. No final evaluator is installed above inquiry.

The authority still targets explanatory universality, not computability. Its substantive relations remain substantive. Explanation, reason use, origin, progress, and capacity have not been reduced to token novelty, output change, graph connectivity, a successful test packet, or an appraisal label. Finite bookkeeping is introduced only in the subordinate specification.

Conjecture still precedes criticism **of that conjecture**. This is not expanded into the false ordering rule that the creative contribution of an entire episode must precede its first criticism. A new explanation of an inherited theory’s defect can arise during the criticism of that theory.

The distinctions between model satisfaction, situated judgment, and attribution evidence are retained and supplemented with an explicit machine-check level. Physical knowledge, explanatory knowledge, and construction capacity remain separate. No claims about consciousness, moral standing, or a universal explainer’s actual existence are inferred from the record structures.

# Findings concerning the semantic authority

## A01 — Later authorship can leak into an earlier origination claim

**Status: formal binding defect; repaired.** The original section “Authorship and distributed contribution” defines `Authors_beta,ell(s,x,p,h)` without an event or contribution index. The definition of OCA then conjoins that history-wide relation with `Attempt(s,x,p,h,e)` and `New_ell,beta(s,x,e,h)` at a particular event.

Consider a system that first deploys a received explanatory account without constructing its organization, then later reconstructs that organization through its own explanatory work. The original formal signature permits the later authorship to make the history-wide `Authors` relation true. It can then combine with the earlier attempted use and earlier newness. The prose clearly intends attribution of the particular contribution, but the formula does not bind it.

The repair adds the contribution index to `Authors` and requires all OCA conjuncts to match the same system, content interpretation, problem, boundary, grain, history, and delimited contribution. An index can delimit extended or distributed work; this does not require an instantaneous cognitive act.

The regression checks matching contribution keys and rejects the cross-event combination. A live mutation removing the contribution index is detected. Matching keys still does not prove that any semantic conjunct is true.

## A02 — Universal inquiry needs an explicit achievement and resource distinction

**Status: underconstrained universal-capacity interpretation; repaired with a substantive strengthening.** The original understanding clause allows communication of an explanation. The creative-inquiry clause also allows genuine reconstruction prompted by a communicated result. Both are reasonable. However, the quantified enabling conditions and the meaning of the required “advance” leave room to substitute a tutor-supplied solution and its reconstruction for a challenge that was supposed to concern originating an unsupplied solution.

This is not presented as a proof that the original UU formula logically entails UC under every admissible interpretation. `Can` and creative advance already carry substantive constraints, and those constraints might block a particular collapse. The defect is that the distinction is not fixed in the challenge itself, so an implementation can equivocate about which achievement its witness establishes.

The replacement makes the achievement respect and permitted environmental contributions part of the independently characterized challenge. Reconstruction of a supplied explanation remains a genuine possible creative achievement. It does not discharge an unsupplied-origination challenge merely because it produces the same final understanding. Full creative-inquiry scope explicitly includes challenges requiring an explanatory contribution not already supplied as the requested solution.

This is the most substantive change to the authority and should be reviewed as such. It strengthens the interpretation of UC rather than merely fixing a type error. It does not demand historical firstness, forbid communication, require identical resources for every problem, or prescribe a computable way of selecting resources.

## A03 — The refinement boundary needs an explicit contract

**Status: missing protection against semantic drift; repaired without defining a decider.** The original repeatedly states that a formal representation must not erase valid prose or tacit roles. Nevertheless, the second document demonstrates how easily a supposedly subordinate kernel can turn a local evidence format into a necessary condition for the phenomenon.

The authority now separates semantic assertions, attribution judgments, record facts, and machine-check facts. A refinement supplies a partial interpretation and explicit bridges. Positive machine classifications do not establish semantic satisfaction without those bridges, and unsupported representations do not establish semantic absence. Both false positives and exclusions of legitimate cases must be examined.

This contract does not require every finite implementation to represent every possible history. It requires the implementation to state what it represents, what it cannot yet represent, and what its results mean. It is an additional methodological modeling commitment, not a theorem that an arbitrary implementation already satisfies it.

## A04 — Uniform premise withdrawal must apply to ordinary content uses

**Status: clarification of an existing authority requirement.** The original “Argument dependencies” section already requires a defeated essential premise to affect the particular argument application using it, without exempting criticisms. The replacement makes explicit that ordinary observations, theories, comparisons, reasons, and provisional assumptions can occupy that role. They need not be specially labeled certificates or standards.

Standing is clarified as use- and respect-indexed where necessary. A premise may be provisionally usable for one qualified argument and unusable for a different application. An attack edge is not itself evidence that a criticism is sound; its grounds, bearing, scope, and activation are separate criticizable judgments.

This is not a new axiom that any objection automatically defeats its target. It protects the original coherence obligation from a representation that quietly omits most premise types.

## A05 — Other clarifications do not change the explanatory target

The authority now explicitly distinguishes actual earlier explanatory ability from an ability the system could acquire after additional learning; recorded causal ancestry from the full history of prior possession; general creative critical connection from a narrow content lineage; and the purported explanatory work of a false variant from an actually adequate explanation.

The refinement contract also distinguishes operational closure from epistemic immunity, duplicate-count invariance from indifference to new reasons, and version traceability from actual recursive return relevance. These repairs make intended constraints harder to evade. They do not supply a full reductive account of `Account`, `Progress`, or `Can`, and no such achievement is claimed.

# Findings concerning the subordinate specification

## S01 — The specification develops a competing authority

**Location: Purpose; Part I; §31. Severity: semantic-authority drift.** The document calls its own Part I the semantic theory and its Part II an admissible interpretation, but does not clearly bind itself to the separately supplied semantic authority. It also adds substantive statements that are not mere restatements, including “the theory has no licence relation,” an exclusive emphasis on refuted-or-not tenability, and various historical claims marked as source or user-adopted commitments.

The repair makes `PopperSemanticsV1_1.md` the explicit normative source. `Licensed_j` is retained as a fallible application appraisal, not misrepresented as an infallible permission. Implementation choices are labeled as choices. Historical comparisons not independently checked are not promoted to project premises.

## S02 — “Nothing closes” conflates fallibility with endless activity

**Location: Purpose; §§8–10; §31. Severity: exclusion of legitimate histories.** Part I allows closed-for-now episodes, but the executable vocabulary prohibits closing and deciding kinds. Constructors also force recordability of criticism from every eligible prefix, while other passages say there is no successor axiom. Some qualifications distinguish recorder from target, but the architecture still obscures the difference between a freely extensible representation and an actual resource-limited system.

A scientist can decide to stop for the day without declaring a result infallible. An archive can cease accepting entries without making its contents true. Conversely, a recorder adding criticism after a target’s death does not show that the target has recursive capacity.

The replacement permits engagement changes and provisional closure. Idealized packet extension is stated as a representation construction, not a guarantee of physical continuation. Actual alternative criticism actions are allowed. Recorder and target are roles that one actor can both occupy.

## S03 — Template eligibility does not ground the template’s other references

**Location: §§8 and 10. Severity: formal contract defect.** `eligible(t,r)` checks that the target exists at the cut, but a criticism template also names a problem, background, standard, grounds, and possibly other versions. The constructor then requires a fresh criticism occurrence whose causes lie in the cut, while grounding requires every payload reference to exist in that causal past.

For a well-formed template with an available target but an unavailable referenced standard, these obligations cannot all be met at that cut unless an unstated restriction repairs eligibility. This exposes an inadmissible prefix/constructor combination; it is not a proof that no model of any restricted instance could exist.

The new contract checks every typed reference. The regression suite catches a missing nested ground and a target-only eligibility mistake. A mutation ignoring nested references is detected.

## S04 — Own-entry references and future references are not separated

**Location: §§8–9; Fixture A. Severity: grounding and replay defect.** The rule that every version named by a payload must already be in the strict past can conflict with references to artifacts created by that same response. At the same time, Fixture A refers to transport `T4` in the response before a later event creates it.

The repair separates external historical references from explicit local references to artifacts created atomically. A response may create both its result and its transport, or a later transport may describe that response. It cannot pretend to have referenced an artifact that did not then exist. Local reference availability does not itself establish the semantic order in which a target was available for criticism.

## S05 — Admitted evidence can count even when its own test failed

**Location: §§11.1–11.4, 15–16 and 19. Severity: false-positive evidence path.** Bodies have validity predicates, and the prose says failed contrasts are invalid. But the raw sets collect admitted certificates, and the adjudicated sets intersect those sets with the grounded extension. The grounded characteristic function checks attacks, not whether the body satisfied its declared finite checks.

A well-formed, admitted certificate recording a failed reason-use experiment is unattacked. It can enter the grounded set and be counted as a positive case through a route whose own experiment failed. The distinction between admission and soundness does not repair this: one must also distinguish admission from satisfaction of the declared witness protocol.

The replacement separates receipt, case formation, finite checks, activation assessment, application usability, and semantic truth. A failed check does not count as passed without waiting for a critic. The material remains recordable and discussable. A different explanatory case can still support the underlying semantic claim. The live failed-body mutation is detected.

## S06 — The argument graph omits ordinary essential premises

**Location: §11.3, especially `Nodes`, `essential`, and `Standing_rec`. Severity: violation of the authority’s dependency requirement.** Nodes are limited to arguments and standards; ordinary conjectures and theories are expressly excluded. Yet certificates may depend essentially on ordinary model assumptions, observations, comparisons, and reasons. Standing is not fully defined for those ordinary premise uses.

A criticism of an ordinary observation interpretation can therefore fail to propagate to a criticism or certificate relying on it. In the fixtures, some essential dependencies name comparison and result contents that are not in the graph’s node domain. A theorem claiming uniform propagation over the restricted graph does not establish uniformity over the authority’s actual premise roles.

The replacement represents **applications and premise uses**, regardless of artifact role. The same content can have distinct scoped uses. Withdrawing an essential observation use disables its dependent criticism just as withdrawing a standard use does. Both cases are tested.

## S07 — Cyclic-policy choices are reported as semantic requirements

**Location: §§5 and 11.3. Severity: policy/authority conflation.** The text describes grounded undecidedness as Part I’s verdict for circular opposition. The authority permits unresolved circular situations but does not say that every cyclic debate must receive one fixed graph-based verdict. Nor does the existence theorem for a finite fixed point show that the selected policy explains reasoned preference.

The replacement names a dependency-aware finite policy, DA-1, and supplies its rule, finite termination argument, and limitations. It requires in status for every essential premise of an in application. Unknown checks do not silently become passing checks. This is a proposed bookkeeping policy, not a theorem that the thinker must reason this way or that other policies are epistemically invalid.

Its finite behavior is tested independently over 2,304 two-node combinations. That test count is evidence about the implementation and small structures, not proof of semantic universality.

## S08 — Record states risk becoming refutation and action rules

**Location: Part I §2.11; §§11.2 and 11.4. Severity: reporting and epistemic drift.** Raw case presence is labeled `STANDING` or `REFUTED`, even though a recorded negative case can be wrong. Other passages suggest that an unrefuted account is the non-arbitrary candidate for action, while the authority explicitly separates unanswered objections from explanatory merit.

The no-accumulation statement also needs a narrower subject: a presence summary is unchanged by adding another case of an already present polarity, but the complete record changes, and a genuinely different argument can matter. Noninductivism is not indifference to the content of evidence.

The replacement uses explicit case-presence labels and retains case identities. A recorded refutation claim is not automatically a successful refutation. Reports expose failed checks, undecided applications, contrary cases, and conditional premises; they do not return a semantic truth verdict.

## S09 — Finite completeness can be confused with semantic completeness

**Location: §§11.4–11.5 and 13. Severity: unjustified positive and negative attributions.** Compound quantifiers range over dossier-named finite ranges, while surrounding claims concern actual earlier repertoires and model domains. A completeness certificate is said to exhaust a sort within a snapshot and not the unrestricted model domain. Without another bridge, the snapshot certificate cannot establish the actual-repertoire universal used for newness.

The replacement separates complete recorded extraction, an explanatory coverage bridge to a semantic domain, and a universal argument. A complete extraction can be checked mechanically. The bridge cannot be obtained merely by scanning more carefully. Missing evidence does not become negation, and a defeated range guard disables a derivation using it.

## S10 — Authorship is narrowed to no equivalent incoming organization

**Location: §16. Severity: direct conflict with K-ORIGIN.** `ValidOrigin` requires that no crossing carry a deployable equivalent of the explanatory organization. The authority explicitly says that authorship is not shorthand for lack of equivalent incoming content and permits a learner to reconstruct a communicated explanation. The specification’s assurance that communication does not disqualify cannot cancel the conjunct that does so.

The repair gives construction, reconstruction, transmission, and external delegation different causal accounts. Equivalent incoming content is not automatically disqualifying. A transmission-only attribution still needs an explanation of why the target did not contribute the claimed organization. The contribution, not the absence of incoming information, carries the burden.

The source check is particularly relevant here: *The Beginning of Infinity*, Chapter 16, treats acquisition of a communicated idea as creating and criticizing explanatory content, rather than passive copying of its meaning.

## S11 — Distributed origin is squeezed into one actor’s trace

**Location: §16. Severity: false negatives and provenance weakness.** Every step of the origin path must have actor `s`, even though the authority allows contributions distributed across a group. Meanwhile external sources, channel descriptions, and a provenance graph are classified as annotations even where they are crucial to deciding authorship.

The replacement requires an integration account for a declared composite and identifies the contributors’ distinct work. Fields are essential or annotative relative to the argument that uses them, not permanently nonessential by name. Merely expanding the boundary is not sufficient.

## S12 — Critical creativity is restricted to an authored target lineage

**Location: §17. Severity: major false-negative classifier.** The CCP witness begins with an OCA, requires a content-parent path from its output to the criticized target, and fixes the criticism’s problem/background to the original pair. This excludes an original explanatory criticism of an inherited theory, an independently originated rival, and several reframed continuations.

The authority’s creative critical contribution may be an explanation of a defect, a reconstructed understanding, a new method, or a new problem formulation. It need not be the ancestor of the first criticized content. The replacement separates a general critical episode, a creative critical episode with a connected OCA, and an optional `LineageCreativeRevision` profile. Content ancestry remains useful where it is actually the relation being claimed.

## S13 — Reason use is identified with an overly strong intervention battery

**Location: §15. Severity: evidence-format overreach.** All four prescribed contrast families must pass, including a family requiring reasoned rejection of invalid criticism. The authority allows genuine but mistaken uptake and does not require a fixed battery of performed interventions. Its relevant counterfactual effects need not change final output.

The replacement makes such a battery an optional stronger protocol property. General reason-use cases need a nonempty, informative contrast account and a causal explanation appropriate to the organization. A failure of the optional battery does not automatically refute `UsesReason`, but a failed result cannot count as a pass through that battery.

## S14 — Good explanations improperly require current-user origination

**Location: §19, `GoodNow_V`. Severity: direct false negative.** `GoodNow_V` requires a valid OCA witness matching the system and explanation. This excludes an inherited explanation that is currently the best relevant account. The authority explicitly allows that case.

The origination requirement is removed from explanatory-quality and current-preference claims. Authorship, possession, quality, and successful knowledge creation remain separate attributions.

## S15 — Progress requires every outcome to fit a candidate-answer envelope

**Location: §19, `Envelope` and `ImprovementBody`. Severity: role/type mismatch and exclusion of progress.** Every outcome must produce an artifact `y`, an adequacy certificate for `y` as an account of the new problem, and a rival-comparison record containing `y`. That can force a new question, standard, rejection, or limitation into the role of a candidate explanation. Some outcome branches then prefer another object while the envelope insists on adequacy for the result object.

The replacement indexes progress to a contribution and whole before/after situations. Outcome-specific bodies explain their particular gain. Candidate adequacy is required when a candidate-adequacy claim is being made, not as a universal property of every result. Criticism rejection, evidence requests, and limitations can support progress when their actual explanatory gain is identified.

Availability also includes the explicitly identified community, not only the original system. A creator’s failure to retain the result does not erase knowledge that the collaboration preserved.

## S16 — Transport coverage is too syntactic and can omit substantive change

**Location: §18. Severity: false positives and restricted expressiveness.** Listing every obligation once in four categories does not establish a valid explanatory transport. The displayed rule demands reasons for abandonment and inapplicability but does not comparably constrain revision mappings. Its unqualified completeness requirement also presupposes a finished obligation inventory.

The replacement requires the role of every named preservation or change to be explained, including the successor of a revised obligation. A finite partition is checked only over a declared range; semantic exhaustiveness has a separate coverage argument. A genuine reframe is neither rejected for abandoning an erroneous demand nor accepted merely for renaming it.

## S17 — The merge view omits the past it is meant to absorb

**Location: §8, `mergedEvents`. Severity: concrete encoding defect.** The forward-only condition selects imported events with `merge_event ≼ imported_event`. This omits contributions that occurred before the merge, even though absorbing those earlier contributions is the central intended use.

The replacement imports selected existing contributions or an earlier snapshot, retaining their identity and location. It does not relocate them to the merge or silently subscribe to future contributions. The regression distinguishes the old forward-only filter from import of an earlier event.

## S18 — Engagement and appraisal views assume a unique temporal maximum

**Location: §§8 and 11.7. Severity: partial-order and missing-evidence defect.** A partial order can have several incomparable maximal events. A definition of engagement in terms of “the maximal event” can be undefined or ambiguous. Upload or record order also cannot by itself determine which situated judgment supersedes another, especially across scopes or concurrent appraisals.

The replacement retains relevant maximal appraisals as a set, with explicit supersession and context. Unknown engagement and conflicting appraisals are representable. A lack of a record is not a negative judgment about the target’s internal state.

## S19 — The capacity formula demands inexhaustible novelty

**Location: §20. Severity: unintended strengthening disguised as capacity.** The original `OCap` requires a fresh valid OCA for each selected problem after every qualifying snapshot. Because OCA includes first possession of new explanatory organization, this can demand endlessly new answers to the same already exhausted problem. It is stronger than reuse of a capacity or open-ended inquiry across new problems.

The replacement separates observed coverage, modal capacity arguments, and optional repeatability hypotheses. Genuine capacity remains substantive and organization-dependent, not a lucky history. Freshness, resource replenishment, and achievement conditions must be declared when a repeatability claim needs them. A two-problem cardinality threshold is not a semantic definition of generality.

## S20 — False explanations can become hard to vary by vacuity

**Location: §21. Severity: concrete false-positive diagnostic.** `FreeVariant_V` requires `Adeq(x′,p,b)`, an actual-account relation. If a false myth has many arbitrary false variants, none need satisfy actual adequacy. The universal `HTV_V` formula can therefore return true despite precisely the arbitrary variation the diagnostic was intended to expose. An empty family also makes the universal true.

The replacement distinguishes preserved **purported explanatory work** from actual adequacy and requires informative material variations. A finite report is scoped to its family and does not claim global rigidity. The regression constructs the false-variant counterexample and the empty-family case. The live empty-family mutation is detected.

## S21 — Reach becomes graph reachability with an unrestricted answer field

**Location: §21, `ReachBody` and `ValidReach`. Severity: insufficient explanatory constraint.** Every obligation can be reached through `transported ∪ added`, but the displayed test does not require those edges to do explanatory work or distinguish ordinary added facts from an added complete answer or creative bridge.

The repair requires an account of the unchanged commitments’ contribution, the relevance of new background, and any newly originated bridge. A graph path remains inspectable evidence, not the definition of explanation. A genuinely new bridge is allowed and receives its own attribution.

## S22 — Fixture A uses a rival from an incompatible branch

**Location: §§27 and 8. Severity: invalid worked configuration.** The original declares `e2` and `e2′` alternatives, creating `X1` and `X1′`. A later comparison names both versions in one actual cut. Grounding and hereditary conflict prevent that cut from being a configuration as described. A theory’s logical incompatibility with a rival does not make the acts of considering them physically incompatible.

The replacement records both rival contents compatibly. Separate tests also retain genuine alternative actions and reject a later transaction whose causal past demands both. This repairs the fixture without imposing semantic acyclicity or suppressing alternatives.

## S23 — The advertised dependency mutation is not exercised by its stated fixture

**Location: §§26–27. Severity: verification overclaim.** The table points to one balances cut, while the fixture prose moves the claimed effect to another. In the later account, a cited objection directly attacks the criticism. Exempting propagation through the criticism’s essential premises would not necessarily remove that direct attack. The fixture lacks the straightforward essential-standard withdrawal needed to establish the claimed test.

The replacement explicitly adds a provisionally accepted objection to the inference-rule application on which the limitation criticism relies. It then checks that the criticism and its dependents lose status, and that a countercriticism can restore them. The live no-exemption mutation is detected. This stress case is clearly identified as a stipulated challenge, not a discovery that the correct balances algebra is false.

## S24 — The proof and verification ledger exceeds what is supplied

**Location: §§23–25, 29 and 33. Severity: unsupported completion claims.** The document states that countermodels are replayed with concrete typed models and all axioms checked, but supplies largely an index and prose fixtures rather than the promised executable or formal artifacts. The physical section says `K_CT` and its bridges are defined and proved there, yet supplies only a deferred-module sketch. Some countermodel rows cite failure of `Nondegenerate`, although that predicate is absent from the weak OCA antecedent/consequent actually at issue. Another appeals to adequacy becoming false later without distinguishing changed appraisal from a changed indexed problem.

The replacement labels definitions, mathematical arguments, evidence-account obligations, optional profiles, and deferred modules. Counterexamples must satisfy the actual antecedent and falsify the actual consequent under one interpretation. Physical bridges remain pending until actually supplied. The attached reference and results establish only their stated finite checks.

# Verification performed

## Reproducible finite results

The package contains a standard-library reference checker, unit and integration tests, two grounded fixture generators, a selected mutation runner, and reports. At the recorded run, **66 tests passed**. One test independently enumerates **2,304** two-node combinations of dependency graphs, attack graphs, and readiness assignments and compares the iterative DA-1 result against enumerated complete labelings. This supplements the written finite-policy argument; it is not a proof of the full semantics.

The integrated fixtures validate record grounding and bind application subjects, activation assessments, and case artifacts to their actual snapshots. The balances fixture has five checked appraisal slices. The seasons fixture has three. Neither fixture infers the truth of `Authors`, `UsesReason`, or `Progress` from passing these checks. The source, basis, and limitations of each stipulated appraisal are explicit.

The mutation runner detects **all nine selected mutations**. They concern failed-body counting, criticism dependency exemption, missing activation, incompatible histories, ungrounded references, erased boundary identity, erased contribution identity, empty variation families, and ignored nested references. A mutation is counted as detected only when the mutated checker compiles and the test execution produces a failing check. Syntax failures and timeouts are not counted as successful detections.

The tests mix representation regressions with explicitly stipulated countermodel demonstrations. The latter show consequences of specified interpretations, not new empirical observations. Their presence should not be described as independent confirmation of the substantive attribution primitives.

## What remains open

The actual causal-semantic adapters for explanation, meaning, reason use, origin, progress, and universal capacity are not completed by this package. The revised specification gives them contracts and discriminating cases rather than fake deciders. Its development gates also require more engineering than the reference subset: persistent receipts and storage, full schema/signature validation, parser adapters, complete version-binding enforcement, and a typed derivation adapter.

There is a written argument for the finite DA-1 policy, along with small exhaustive checks, but no proof-assistant formalization of the whole specification. The policy remains replaceable and may prove inadequate for some forms of mutually constraining inquiry. Its very purpose is to be a declared, criticizable bookkeeping interpretation, not an unquestionable evaluator.

The positive and negative sides of each semantic adapter still need adversarial explanatory testing. For example, preventing a copier from receiving authorship credit is not enough; the adapter must also avoid excluding a learner’s genuine reconstruction. Preventing renamed problems from counting as solutions is not enough; a legitimate explanatory revolution must remain representable.

## Recommended adoption boundary

The narrow authorship-index repair and the specification’s direct conflicts with the authority can be evaluated against concrete counterexamples now. The achievement-sensitive strengthening of UC should be considered explicitly rather than hidden among mechanical fixes. Adopting the package adopts a clearer proposed authority and a subordinate staged path—not a claim that a universal creative system has been implemented.

The next engineering target is a stored dossier and parser feeding the tested record/appraisal layer. The next semantic target is a genuinely explanatory adapter for one attribution relation, challenged with both exclusions and false positives. Those are parallel kinds of progress. Making the bookkeeping executable does not solve the semantic attribution problem, and the semantic problem does not make careful bookkeeping pointless.
