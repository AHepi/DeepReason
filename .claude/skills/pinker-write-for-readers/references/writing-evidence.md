# Evidence and attribution for Pinker-informed writing

Research current through 2026-09-03.

## Core model

Pinker treats writing as a cognitive design problem. A reader must reconstruct a conceptual web through syntactic structure presented as a linear word string. The writer therefore has to choose a communication stance, supply context hidden by expertise, manage real-time processing, and connect sentences into a coherent whole. Usage matters, but not more than factual accuracy, reasoning, audience, coherence, and clarity.

## Primary Pinker sources

- [Official page for *The Sense of Style*](https://stevenpinker.com/publications/sense-style-thinking-persons-guide-writing-21st-century) establishes the book's scope.
- [“Writing in the 21st Century,” Edge](https://www.edge.org/conversation/steven_pinker-writing-in-the-21st-century) is the strongest open first-person synthesis of classic style, psycholinguistics, passive voice, usage, reading, feedback, and revision.
- [“Why Academics' Writing Stinks,” Chronicle of Higher Education](https://www.chronicle.com/article/why-academics-stink-at-writing/) treats metadiscourse, hedging, nominalizations, jargon, the curse of knowledge, and revision.
- [“The Source of Bad Writing,” author-hosted page](https://stevenpinker.com/publications/source-bad-writing?preview=true) focuses on audience knowledge gaps and revision.
- [“Passive Resistance,” The Atlantic](https://www.theatlantic.com/magazine/archive/2014/11/passive-resistance/380787/) explains why passive voice is sometimes clearest.
- [“10 grammar rules it's OK to break (sometimes),” The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/books/2014/aug/15/steven-pinker-10-grammar-rules-break) gives an operational framework for usage disputes.
- [Harvard Gazette on the curse of knowledge](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2012/11/exorcising-the-curse-of-knowledge/) emphasizes representative readers, concrete explanation, and reverse-engineering good prose.
- [Harvard Gazette on clarity](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2015/03/bringing-sanity-to-clarity/) emphasizes abstraction, revision, and putting the reader's needed order ahead of the writer's discovery order.
- [Ploughshares interview](https://pshares.org/blog/an-interview-with-steven-pinker/) distinguishes syntactic difficulty from sentence length and stresses fit among audience, medium, and register.
- [2025 interview transcript](https://howiwrite.substack.com/p/steven-pinker-rules-for-writing-and) covers generalization-example pairing, reading aloud, compression, reader modeling, and AI banality.
- [Harvard Gazette on ChatGPT](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2023/02/will-chatgpt-replace-human-writers-pinker-weighs-in/) separates linguistic fluency from world understanding and raises hallucination and source-traceability concerns.

## Evidence boundary

Pinker combines linguistic analysis, cognitive research, lexicography, historical usage, close reading, and aesthetic judgment. Treat the result as evidence-informed heuristics, not a collection of universal experimental laws. A scholarly review discussing limits of some empirical claims is [Stephan Meyer, DOI 10.18552/joaw.v5i2.229](https://doi.org/10.18552/joaw.v5i2.229).

Classic style is one communication stance, not a guarantee of truth or a universal genre. Clarity cannot rescue false facts or a defective argument, and confident presentation must never erase real uncertainty.
