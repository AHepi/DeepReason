Generated by `python3 tables.py` from `road_census.json` and `trial_sweep.json`. Every gate is transcribed from source order in `rules/crit.py::_crit_argumentative_batch_result` (leg 1) and `informal/trial.py::_argument_trial_steps` (leg 2); every count is a typed marker in `log.jsonl` or a warrant in `objects/`.

**Read leg 1 and leg 2 differently, because the record supports them differently.** Leg 2 is an EXACT CHAIN: every trial that enters `_argument_trial_steps` leaves by exactly one door, so the remainder column is a subtraction and closes on the minted count. Leg 1 is NOT a chain: its stages are measured independently — dispatches and cases from each critic call's own raw completion blob, terminators from their typed Measures — and they need not reconcile to the row, because a batch can name a target twice or name one outside the batch (both `continue` with no marker at all), and a completion blob that will not parse is counted unreadable rather than dropped. Where the two disagree the gap is printed, never smoothed.
