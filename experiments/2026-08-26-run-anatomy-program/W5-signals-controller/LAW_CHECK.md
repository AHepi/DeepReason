## What is actually tested

The exemption is by exact artifact id and names the family it belongs to, so it stays auditable instead of being a blanket over anything a `Refl` event touched.

## What this check CANNOT establish

It is a structural check, not a correlation test. It proves no signal emission or allocation decision CARRIED a foreign label change in its own event. It does not prove that no label change was downstream-influenced by one, and with 27 decision cycles in the whole population there is no statistical power for a correlation test — a per-cycle label-change rate compared across decision and non-decision cycles would be noise reported as a finding. The structural check is the guarantee the design actually makes, and it holds; the correlational claim is not made.

The stronger evidence for the same boundary is not in this table at all: it is the decision-and-effect table's `wire` column. A controller whose caps never reached a dispatch could not have influenced any label even if the boundary were unguarded.
