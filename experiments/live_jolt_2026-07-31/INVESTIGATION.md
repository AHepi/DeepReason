## What is broken

**(1) Integrity — cause established, READER side.** `_decomposition_merge_admits` (`src/deepreason/invariants.py:503`) is the only thing that can admit a decomposition-merge Conj event, and its per-child gate at `invariants.py:534-545` demands that *every* entry of the completion's `child_work_ids` be a preparation whose payload carries `schema == "contract-decomposition-child.v1"` and `decomposition_transition_ref == transition.id`. When an atomic child is rejected and repaired, the writer names the repair work item instead — `schema='repair.semantic-task.v1'`, `decomposition_transition_ref=None`, `parent_work_id` pointing at the rejected child. That happened at slot-000 in cycles 1 and 2 (completions `62b5e32458f8` → Conj seq 245, `f8335acf8f40` → Conj seq 386) and not in cycle 0 (`3340c059d828` → Conj seq 110). The gate returns False at index 0 and the finding fires. Three independent skeptics stepped the clause chain and all three landed on the same single clause; none could find a second failing gate.

Two things in the brief need correcting. First, **seq 110 does not pass the primary admission test either** — all three atomic-contract Conj events (110, 245, 386) yield `matching_admissions == 0` at `invariants.py:623-629`; only the two `conjecturer.turn.v6` events (482, 747) yield 1. Second, the hypothesis's writer-side argument cited the wrong raise: `Harness.complete_contract_decomposition` filters candidates by `terminal.status == "completed"` at `harness.py:1377-1383` *before* the admission check at `:1385-1387`, and the root children are `rejected`, so forcing one into `child_work_ids` trips `decomposition child lacks one completed result`, not the admission error. It named its own falsifier — a single v6-text warrant from any other commitment family — and that warrant is on record. `experiments/live_compare_2026-07-28/deepseek/shallow-runs/shallow-dc6fe3f9c26cede686906a16` is `schema_version=6`, `workload_profile=text`, and its `log.jsonl` seq 18 is a Crit event carrying `w:skeleton-wf:a246c8f7…` with a real attack edge and final state `{ACCEPTED: 27, REFUTED: 1}` (I re-read the record myself). ## Why the root is not replay-valid

**READER.** The mechanism runs one way only. The writer's own replay validator *requires* the shape the reader rejects: `workflow/replay.py:565-598` builds per-slot candidates as `{root child} ∪ {its repair.semantic-task.v1 descendants}`, takes the single completed item, and errors with `decomposition completion child inventory differs` unless `completion.child_work_ids` equals exactly those results. `replay.py:713-723` has a dedicated `repair.semantic-task.v1` branch that recurses to `parent_work_id` after checking contract id, route lease and target refs. `invariants.py` itself follows the same repair→parent convention in two other places (`_prepared_school_id` at `:647-660`, `_is_patch_repair_semantic_rejection` at `:704-735`). The merge gate at `:534-545` is the only place in the chain that assumes homogeneity — and its own docstring claims it enforces "the same join the replay validator enforces," which is exactly what it does not do.

**Existing replay-valid roots are not at risk.** Three separate differentials agree: patching only that gate to walk `parent_work_id` while the payload is a repair takes this root from 2 findings to 0 and moves nothing else. Denominators differ across the three runs (42 roots via `verify_root`, 27 via a Harness-mediated sweep, 19 in a completions-only scan) and I flag that rather than smooth it: 11 of the 42 `log.jsonl` roots under `experiments/` cannot be opened by today's `Harness` at all (`UnsupportedRunManifestVersionError`, all pre-v6 — `bronze_repertoire_v2`, `bronze_pilot`, `glm_judge`, `gemma4_dna_*`, `jolt_architecture`, `bronze_feedback_v1_superseded`). Any Harness-based corpus claim in either track covers 31 roots, not 42. The `verify_root`-level differential does cover 42 and reports 41 unchanged. The shape itself is rare: exactly 2 decomposition completions in the whole corpus name a non-child member, both in this root.

Attribution of the write is unambiguous from the record: `REPLAY_VALIDATION.json`'s `verification.violations` contains exactly these two findings and nothing else. The run flagged itself at write time; today's reader agrees with the reader of 2026-08-01.

## Why nothing was adjudicated

**World (c), defect — but not the defect proposed, and split in two.**

World (a), as-designed budget exhaustion, is refuted by the record: adjudication is not a budget-gated phase. `Harness._adjudicate()` recomputes status from the attack graph after every event (`harness.py:2111-2113` → `:2132-2146`), and `progress.jsonl` seqs 2-13 show accepted climbing 62 → 72 while it ran. More budget buys more conjecture, not more adjudication.

World (b), starvation, is refuted three ways: 30 criticism assignments, 30 attempts, all `completed`, and every `criticism-coverage-debt-v1` record reads `coverage_complete`; the budget denials arrived at cycles 4-5 after coverage was already complete and hit 5 conjecture orders against 1 criticism order; and the `live_engaged` root had *worse* criticism coverage (6 of 35 attempts budget-denied) and still produced a refutation.

The discriminating evidence is the attack-edge precondition. An attack exists iff a registered artifact carries a warrant whose target is registered (`adjudication/edges.py:98-114`), and unattacked nodes are vacuously accepted (`grounded.py:20`). Across all 851 events, `state_diff['att+']` and `state_diff['carry+']` are empty in every one. Not one attack was attempted. Why: `authority.py:97-101` computes the trial mode and then hard-returns `TrialAuthority.OBSERVE_ONLY` for every text workload, so all 27 Crit events route to `_observe_case` and register prose critics with no warrants; the v6 deferral at `scheduler.py:482-538` fired 42 times (36 hv-spot-check, 6 hv-floor); 82 of the run's commitments are `program:reasoning_observation_pending`, which returns `OVERRUN` unconditionally (`programs.py:220-222`) with no upgrade path anywhere in `src/`; `reasoning-envelope-wf` is tautological on candidates whose content *is* `envelope_json(envelope)`; and the seed named nothing that could fail.

So there are two real defects here, not one:

- **A reporting defect, cheap and certain.** `run-status.json`'s `accepted=0 refuted=0` are pydantic defaults — the terminal emit at `text_runs.py:1095-1108` passes no such kwargs (defaults at `runtime/progress.py:47-49`). The same file reports `display_status_counts: {"standing": 72}`, and `status_display.py:27-28` renames ACCEPTED to "standing" for text. **standing=72 means 72 ACCEPTED.** Likewise `run-stop.json`'s metrics are a fresh `StopMetrics(cycle=…)` (`text_runs.py:1031-1032`) — the true `criticism_debt` recomputed on the final state is 0.176, not 0.0; `queued_checks` is the literal 0 (`scheduler.py:2506-2508`); `queued_criticism` has no writer anywhere in `src/`; `determinate=True` appears nowhere. Every field the brief cites as a symptom is a constant. `budget_exhausted` is itself the fall-through label applied when the scheduler returned no stop reason (`text_runs.py:1022-1027`).
- **A blindness defect, promoted from the third skeptic and stronger than the hypothesis's own framing.** The design *does* treat zero attacks as a pathology — `docs/harness-spec-v1.3.md:446`: "if no test is ever attacked, D3 has died in practice while remaining true on paper." The implemented detector cannot fire in that extreme: `capture/detection.py:319-327` needs 2 of 4 conditions, two are guarded behind `MIN_ATTACKS_FOR_RITUAL=5`, `attack_target_entropy` is `None` with no attacks, and `criticism_debt` 0.176 sits under the 0.5 ceiling. Measured on this root, `ritual_conditions == [False, False, False, False]`. The companion alarm reads green vacuously: `grounding_lambda` is 1.0 by the documented "no verdicts in window ⇒ 1.0" rule. `run-result.json` reports `epistemic_checks_passed: true` with 0 epistemic findings on a run that could not falsify anything.

The design-intent skeptic is right that a text run stopping underdetermined is documented as a valid outcome (`README.md:321`, `docs/AGENT.md:177-179`), and that `observe_only` is deliberate policy pending a calibration-receipt verifier (`authority.py:96-99`). That defends the *outcome*. It does not defend the record's silence about it.
