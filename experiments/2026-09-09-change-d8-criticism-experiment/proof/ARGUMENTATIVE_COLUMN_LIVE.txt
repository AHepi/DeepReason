=== Step 10a: the census's argumentative column can move — and why the proof
    has to come from a fixture ===

THE MEASUREMENT THAT MADE THIS STEP NECESSARY (2026-09-09, over the 20 most
criticism-heavy committed roots, ranked by W2's own roots.json):

    warrants by road: {'demonstrative': 1141}
    roots with ANY argumentative warrant: NONE

Not one argumentative warrant has ever been minted in this repository's
committed corpus. The 2026-09-08 audit reported this shape for the two roots
it examined closely; this scan says it holds across the twenty heaviest, and
step 9 found it again on the organiser tranche's own terminal root (1 attack
edge, 1 demonstrative warrant, 0 argumentative).

WHY THAT IS A PROBLEM FOR THIS TRANCHE SPECIFICALLY. ARM A exists to find out
whether a granted authority lets a model's case move a status. Its answer is
read off exactly the column that has never, in this repository's history,
returned anything but zero. A zero from ARM A would therefore be
indistinguishable from an instrument that cannot return anything else — which
is not a measurement.

THE POSITIVE CONTROL, on a fixture, since no committed root can provide one:

    $ python -m pytest tests/test_record_census.py -q
    ...                                                                  [100%]
    3 passed

MUTATION PROOF — the tests can fail, and fail for the reason they claim.
Collapsing the two roads into one (the exact defect that would make the
corpus's 1 141 demonstrative warrants read as criticism that moved a status):

    --- with the roads collapsed ---
    FAILED tests/test_record_census.py::test_the_argumentative_column_moves_when_an_argumentative_warrant_exists
    1 failed, 2 passed
    --- restored ---
    3 passed

REGISTERED FOR TRANCHE 2, so it is not decided after the fact: if ARM A
terminates cleanly and its argumentative warrant count is 0, that is a
FINDING about granted authority on this configuration — not a broken
instrument and not a failed arm. This proof is what licenses that reading.
