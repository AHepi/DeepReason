**Offline, ARM A is INDISTINGUISHABLE from ARM C.** No judge fired, no

authority road is ARMED and never TRAVELLED here.

So the soak establishes that ARM A's SHAPE drives to a clean terminal, and
nothing whatever about whether granted authority does anything. That the road is
REACHABLE was established separately and statically (step 28,
`proof/ARM_A_ROUTE_IDENTITY.txt`): `_authority` over ARM A's own compiled
manifest returns `trial_required` rather than `observe_only`;
`require_cross_family_judge_ensemble` returns two seats of two families; and ARM
C — single-family — is REFUSED, which is the control proving that check can say
no.

Whether the road is TRAVELLED is what tranche 2 measures, and PREREG §12 already
registers what a zero there would mean: a finding about granted authority, not a
broken instrument. This proof is part of why that reading is licensed — the
instrument's ability to report a non-zero argumentative count is proved on a
fixture with a mutation, because no root in this repository can prove it.
