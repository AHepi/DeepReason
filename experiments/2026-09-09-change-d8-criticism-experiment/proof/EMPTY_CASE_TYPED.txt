(roots.json lists the 60 committed roots that carry criticism; this is
one of the 30 that do not). Chosen by that census rather than by
inspection, so the choice is not the author's.

Read it plainly: n=0 and every rate column is an em dash. A rate over an
empty denominator is not 0.0% -- it does not exist, and printing 0.0%
would invent evidence the record does not carry. This matters for the
tranche's own ARM V and ARM A: an arm that dies before any criticism is
written must be reported as unmeasured, never as measured-at-zero.
