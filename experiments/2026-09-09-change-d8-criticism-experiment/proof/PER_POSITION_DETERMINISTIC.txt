This matters more than it looks. The primary comparison is judged on the
composed unit; the secondary one (R19) is judged on its positions. If the
edit that added the second had moved the first, the two comparisons would
be reading different things and the secondary could not be the check on
the primary that it is meant to be. One renderer, _position_block, is
called by both paths, so a per-position unit is byte-identical to its own
paragraph inside the composed unit.
