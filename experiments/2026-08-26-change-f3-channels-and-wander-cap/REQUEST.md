## Open questions (for dr-spec-change)

Q1: "coding" (R3) — which channel does the operator mean? Two readings are
live on this tree: (a) the code-testing/execution channel of the 2026-08-14
ruling (`programs.py` / `oracle.py`, `candidate_checker`, `exec_oracle`), which
appears to have no enablement gate at all today; (b) model-authored Python
inside the simulation capability, which IS gated —
`DEEPREASON_SIMULATION_RUNNER=contained` is required and the default
`declarative` runner "refuses `sandboxed_python_v1` proposals outright". The
readings differ materially in effort and in blast radius.

Q2: research needs a FROZEN DOMAIN ALLOWLIST to be enabled at all
(`ResearchCapabilityPolicyV1` raises "enabled research requires a frozen domain
allowlist"). "Permanently on" therefore requires a DEFAULT allowlist. What
should it be, and does an empty-allowlist "on" state (enabled, zero reachable
hosts) count as on?

Q3: what is the floor's default VALUE (R8)? The instruction declares the knob
but not the number; W6 measured 53.2 % actual on the seed lineage.

Q4: does "deprioritized" (R9) mean rank demotion only, or may a self-spawned
lineage be refused a cycle outright once the floor is threatened? R9's "by the
existing attention/allocation machinery" constrains the mechanism but not the
strength.

Q5: R13's fork — emit or strike — is chosen per signal. Which of the four
phantoms does the shipped policy actually consume?
