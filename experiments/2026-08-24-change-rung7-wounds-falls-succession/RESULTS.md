# RESULTS — Rung 7: wounds, falls, and succession

Honest ledger, dated segments. What the record shows, and the residue — what
remains unproven. Accepted does not mean true.

## 2026-08-24 — the tranche, offline

**What was built, and what deliberately was not.**

Wounds: nothing. That was the requirement, not a shortcut. A fail verdict on
the subject's own observation-valued commitment already yields a demonstrative
warrant, an attack edge and a refuted label through the tree's one warrant
constructor. Rung 7 owed a PROOF that standing is untouched by it, and the
proof is taken at every layer a wound could have reached — the label moves, the
assertion's own label does not, the grant is identical field for field, the
frame still renders with the wound on display, and the cascade does not fire.

That last clause is where the mutation proof bites. A clause making a refuted
subject lose its own standing turns three of the six red. The three it does NOT
turn red are exactly the ones a label-only test would have been satisfied by,
which is the argument for checking the grant, the render and the cascade as
well as the label.

**The cascade's second entry, and the shape of "no second mechanism".** A
consulted frame assertion that has left unrefuted standing marks every problem
it carried. The entries differ in what they READ and in nothing else: both
produce `(problem, label)` pairs and both pass through the same grading step.
The absence of a second mechanism is asserted structurally, because no
behavioural test can see one — exactly one function in `src/` names a cascade
grade constant.

Three conditions the frame entry keeps, and two of them are the interesting
ones. Separation, through Rung 3b's own predicate, so an assertion that was
never consultable orphans nothing (R64 gains a fourth clause: no edge, no
warrant, no label, **and no mark**). And the exclusion of the assertion's OWN
promotion problem — σ can admit it by accident, because the two are about the
same subject, and marking it would deprioritize in scheduling the one problem
D-1 requires to stay on the frontier when a frame falls.

**Succession changed nothing about how a rivalry arrives.** The discrimination
spawn is untouched and knows nothing about frames. What Rung 7 adds is what the
pack SHOWS — one suppression site, both articulation digests, both candidates'
wounds under the same stated cap, ordered by subject id rather than by
incumbency — and what the trial RECORDS.

**The residue, stated as §13 states it.** Verbatim from
`docs/COMPUTABLE_CALCULUS.md` §13:

> And a wounded background with no arriving rival frames forever — refuted,
> indicted in every pack, unreplaced, and never declared irreplaceable (N3).

This tranche does not change that and could not have. The cascade fires, the
crisis renders, the promotion problem rises with its wound count, and the
succession trial is ready the moment a rival arrives. None of that can make a
rival arrive. "The calculus keeps the crisis visible and the succession problem
open; it cannot force the successor into existence."

## What remains unproven

1. **Every succession proof in this tranche is OFFLINE.** No live run has yet
   produced two rival frame assertions on one promotion problem, so the
   succession pack and the trial record have never been exercised by a model.
   The order-disagreement case that proves the no-verdict road is CONSTRUCTED —
   a stubbed judge that names the same presented slot twice. That the road
   works is proven; that a live judge takes it is not.

2. **The flip rate has no measured value yet.** Q2's own number — ordering
   alone flips the top-1 candidate on 16–39% of prompts — comes from six
   open-weight judges on four datasets, not from this harness. The instrument
   that would let this harness measure its own now exists and reports on every
   trial; what it will say here is unknown.

3. **The program road's discriminator is narrow, and visibly so.** By the time
   a succession trial exists both rivals have already passed `accounts-for`, so
   the program separates them only where the record already does — a candidate
   declaring wounds the other does not. Everything else answers `neither` and
   routes onward. That is D-6 answer A working as specified rather than a gap,
   but it means the SOLO road will usually reach no verdict, and a solo run's
   succession will in practice wait for a rival that accounts for strictly more.

4. **Limb 2 of `cascade-integrity` has never fired on a real record**, and
   cannot without a mutation, because it re-derives the same obligation the
   marking function satisfies. Its value is as a tripwire on a future change,
   not as a finding about today's roots. The test that proves it CAN fire is
   structural.

5. **A separation lost after consultation is disclosed, not repaired.**
   Components only grow, so an assertion separated when it was consulted can be
   unseparated later, and the frame entry is then silent for it. Limb 3 reports
   it; nothing decides what should happen next, and nothing has yet observed
   the case on a live root.
