and 11, under rules 90, 110 and 184. Question: for which (rule, W) pairs does
every initial state reach a cycle of length at most 2 within 4*W steps, and
what structural property of the rule separates the pairs that do from the
pairs that do not? Candidates must include a runnable Python entry point
solve(rule, width) returning {"all_short_cycles": bool, "witness": [...]}
where witness is a concrete initial state achieving the longest observed
transient. Claims about exhaustiveness are claims about enumeration: file
typed sandboxed Python simulations (simulation_mode sandboxed_python_v1) that
RETURN the per-(rule,width) transient and cycle-length tables your claim
rests on - never a summary boolean. A separating property that is merely
renamed data is worth little; predict the behaviour of rule 54, which is NOT
in the required set, and state how the prediction would be checked.
