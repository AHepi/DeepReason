## Question

This is the third-seed variant of the same question: Argue for or against the claim that a panel of trained non-experts following an explicit checklist will, on average, produce more reliable judgments than a single domain expert relying on intuition, for tasks with objectively verifiable outcomes. Identify what evidence within a single evaluation would distinguish a genuine reliability gain from mere checklist compliance that happens to track the right answer.
